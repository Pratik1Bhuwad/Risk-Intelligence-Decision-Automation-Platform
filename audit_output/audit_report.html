6. Synthetic-Data Artifact Review

Hypothesis ID Hypothesis Description Empirical Evidence Interpretation Status
H1_FRAUD_TRANSACTION_TYPES Fraud occurs exclusively in TRANSFER and CASH_OUT transaction types. Observed fraud types: ['CASH_OUT', 'TRANSFER']. Fraud occurrences outside TRANSFER/CASH_OUT: 0. Fraud generation in PaySim simulation is strictly restricted to TRANSFER and CASH_OUT. Models can eliminate fraud predictions on PAYMENT, CASH_IN, and DEBIT. CONFIRMED
H2_FRAUD_BALANCE_DRAINING Fraud transactions drain the entire available balance (amount == oldbalanceOrg and newbalanceOrig == 0). 8018 of 8213 fraud transactions (97.63%) have amount == oldbalanceOrg. 8053 (98.05%) result in zero newbalanceOrig. High concentration (97.63%) of fraud events transfer exactly the full balance, indicating a synthetic simulation rule where simulated fraudsters liquidate compromised accounts completely. OBSERVED
H3_MERCHANT_BALANCE_MECHANICS Merchant accounts (nameDest starting with 'M') always have zero balances (oldbalanceDest == 0 and newbalanceDest == 0). Of 2151495 merchant destination records, 2151495 (100.0%) have both balances exactly equal to 0. Merchant destination balances are not simulated in PaySim and remain fixed at 0. Balance features for merchants convey no information and reflect simulator omission. CONFIRMED
H4_ORIGIN_BALANCE_DISCREPANCY Originating account balance transition exhibits arithmetic discrepancies (old - amount != new). 3678407 of 4963336 non-cash-in records (74.11%) violate exact balance accounting. Balance tracking in the simulator contains intentional or unintentional accounting errors, such as transactions allowed from zero-balance accounts or truncated calculations. OBSERVED
H5_ORIGIN_ACCOUNT_UNIQUENESS Originating accounts appear nearly or strictly once in the entire dataset, precluding customer history depth. 6353307 unique originating accounts across 6362620 rows (99.8536% unique). In PaySim, originating accounts rarely repeat. This confirms that within-customer behavioral profiling is severely limited on the origin side without external customer identity linkage. CONFIRMED
H6_DESTINATION_ACCOUNT_REUSE Destination accounts receive repeat transactions, allowing destination-level profiling. 2722362 unique destination accounts across 6362620 rows (reuse rate 57.2132%). Destination entities exhibit substantial repeat activity, enabling entity-level destination profiling, in-degree velocity metrics, and merchant risk categorization. CONFIRMED
H7_FLAGGED_FRAUD_RULE_LOGIC isFlaggedFraud represents a simple threshold rule: TRANSFER transactions > 200,000. Flagged count: 16. Types: ['TRANSFER']. Min amount: 353874.22. All > 200k: True. Unflagged transfers > 200k: 409094. All 16 flagged records are TRANSFERs > 200,000, but 409094 transfers > 200k were NOT flagged. The rule is either an incomplete/legacy threshold or depends on an undocumented condition. OBSERVED

Transaction Risk Score Observed: complete transaction records with type, amount, oldbalanceOrg, nameDest prefixes, and 8,213+ fraud labels. Customer Risk Score Originating accounts (nameOrig) appear nearly 100% uniquely (single transaction per originator). In contrast, destination accounts (nameDest) show repeat activity. SEVERELY_CONSTRAINED Customer risk scoring cannot be built on originating accounts within PaySim due to absence of historical origin activity. Feasible only for destination entities (recipient/merchant risk profiling) or via external identity linkage.
Behavioral Change Indicators Single-transaction origin accounts prevent customer-level baseline derivation across time. SEVERELY_CONSTRAINED Within-customer behavioral deviation is impossible on originating accounts. Macro-level or destination-level velocity (e.g. surge in incoming transfers to a single destination account) can still be computed.

FEASIBLE Explanations must clearly reflect the limitations of the data (e.g. noting that originator has no prior transaction history in the system).
